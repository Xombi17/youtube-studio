## 1. Premise & Dramatic Arc

Grand Theft Auto VI requires unprecedented streaming throughput from console storage. This film takes apart the PS5 storage architecture layer by layer in a continuous orbit, pinpointing exactly where the official 150 GB+ installation files must reside, why legacy external USB drives cannot run the game, and how to prepare your console.

## 2. Structure & Three Candidates

- **Option A (Sequential Install Guide):** Cover removal $\rightarrow$ slot access $\rightarrow$ drive screw $\rightarrow$ boot check.
- **Option B (The Exploded Teardown — Selected):** Camera orbits an assembled PS5 console. The side plates lift on the X-axis, revealing the cooling fan and the metallic M.2 expansion bay cover. The cover slides open; an illuminated PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD floats outward into the macro lens with glowing dimension leaders indicating "PCIe Gen4 x4: 5,500 MB/s minimum" and "150 GB GTA VI Cache Target".
- **Option C (Abstract Spec Comparison):** Split comparison of internal vs external speeds.
