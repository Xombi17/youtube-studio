## Tips (Script-safe, sourced)

1. **Free space first** — confirm 212 GB free before preload begins
2. **Delete old games** — saves can remain in PS5 cloud storage
3. **External USB drive** — useful for game saves but NOT for running PS5 games
4. **M.2 expansion slot** — fastest way to add permanent storage; must be PS5-compatible NVMe SSD (PCIe Gen 4 recommended)
5. **Start preload early** — download is ~210 GB; on average home broadband it can take many hours
