## Beat-by-Beat Breakdown

### Beat 01 — Hook (`hook`)
- **Spoken:** "Twenty percent less food does not mean twenty percent pricier. It actually costs you twenty-five percent more."
- **Visual:** Dark Navy (`#0E1F2A`). Slam headline `20% SMALLER.` → yellow reveal `25% PRICIER.`. Side-by-side bespoke vector kraft snack bags (`200g` vs `160g`) with translucent fill-line indicators and taped yellow `$4` price tag hanging between them.
- **SFX:** `sfx_paper_rip.wav`

### Beat 02 — Baseline Unit Price (`baseline`)
- **Spoken:** "Here's the sneaky math. Say a snack bag costs four dollars for two hundred grams. Divide that out, and you're paying two cents per gram."
- **Visual:** Warm Newsprint Cream (`#F3EBD8`). 200g snack bag resting on an animated vintage mechanical kitchen scale (needle sweeps to `200g`), hanging `$4.00` price tag, torn-paper equation card `$4.00 ÷ 200g = 2.0¢ / gram` with yellow highlighter wipe.
- **SFX:** `sfx_tape_snap.wav`

### Beat 03 — The Silent Shrink (`shrink`)
- **Spoken:** "Next month, the price tag doesn't budge, still four dollars. But quietly, the bag drops to one hundred sixty grams."
- **Visual:** Warm Cream (`#F3EBD8`), seamless match-cut! The `$4.00` tag stays locked in place while a bold red `LOCKED` stamp slams onto it. The bag smoothly shrinks on the scale as the red dial needle drops from `200g` down to `160g`.
- **SFX:** `sfx_stamp_thud.wav`

### Beat 04 — Missing Fifth (`missing_fifth`)
- **Spoken:** "That's twenty percent less food inside, two out of every ten bites gone, while the checkout total looks identical."
- **Visual:** Archival Kraft (`#EFE4CC`). 10-block tactile portion bar (`10 × 20g`). Blocks 9 & 10 tear off along perforated lines and tumble away with a `-20%` coral badge, paired with ghosted bag headspace comparison.
- **SFX:** `sfx_paper_rip.wav`

### Beat 05 — Receipt Math (`receipt_math`)
- **Spoken:** "Now run the unit price again. Four dollars divided by one hundred sixty grams jumps to two and a half cents per gram."
- **Visual:** Deep Navy (`#0E1F2A`). Upright serrated thermal grocery receipt unrolls smoothly from a top slot, printing monospace lines (`PRICE $4.00`, `WEIGHT 160 g`, `$4.00 ÷ 160 g`), then slamming a coral stamp: `2.5¢ / GRAM`.
- **SFX:** `sfx_cash_ding.wav`

### Beat 06 — The +25% Payoff (`payoff_chart`)
- **Spoken:** "Going from two cents to two point five isn't a twenty percent hike. That's a full twenty-five percent price increase."
- **Visual:** Deep Navy (`#0E1F2A`) with high-contrast Coral & Yellow. Giant headline crosses out `+20%` and slams **`+25%`**! Below: zero-baseline Vox comparison bar chart (`2.0¢/g` vs `2.5¢/g`, exact 2:2.5 pixel height ratio) with dashed baseline reference and `+0.5¢ (+25%)` bracket.
- **SFX:** `impact.wav`

### Beat 07 — Denominator Mechanics (`denominator`)
- **Spoken:** "Why the mismatch? Because shrinking the package shrinks the denominator, and dividing by a smaller number pushes the real cost up faster."
- **Visual:** Warm Cream (`#F3EBD8`). Large taped blueprint equation card showing the live fraction `$4.00 / [200g → 160g]` compressing downward on the bottom while an upward indicator pushes the unit cost higher.
- **SFX:** `sfx_tape_snap.wav`

### Beat 08 — Shrinkflation on the Shelf (`shrinkflation_rule`)
- **Spoken:** "Economists at the Bureau of Labor Statistics call this shrinkflation, and it hides right on the shelf."
- **Visual:** Bold Editorial Ochre (`#F7D046`). Giant headline `SHRINKFLATION` + red rubber stamp `BLS.GOV // INFLATION`. Below: realistic supermarket shelf label strip with an animated magnifying glass zooming onto the tiny corner `UNIT PRICE: 2.5¢ / g` box next to the giant `$4.00`.
- **SFX:** `sfx_stamp_thud.wav`

### Beat 09 — Takeaway & Loop (`takeaway`)
- **Spoken:** "So next time you shop, ignore the big sticker. Check the unit price per gram. Same four dollars, completely different deal."
- **Visual:** Signature Navy Finale (`#0E1F2A`). Side-by-side verdict cards (`200g • 2.0¢/g` vs `160g • 2.5¢/g • +25%`) beneath bold `SAME $4. DIFFERENT DEAL.` banner.
- **SFX:** `sfx_cash_ding.wav`
