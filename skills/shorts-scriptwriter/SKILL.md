# Shorts scriptwriter

Ask for or infer the audience, subject, target duration, language and tone. Read the claim ledger when facts matter. Write a spoken hook that delivers the premise immediately, then one idea per beat with a clear payoff. Give each beat a rough time range, voice line, on-screen text, and visual purpose. Keep on-screen text readable in vertical safe areas and write natural speech rather than dense paragraphs.

Read the script aloud or estimate speaking time before delivery; trim until it fits. End with a payoff or natural loop, not a generic engagement request. Mark any unverified line for research before production. Deliver the script plus a one-sentence thumbnail/title angle; do not promise performance metrics.
