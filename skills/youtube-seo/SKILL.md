## Purpose
Produce a complete `metadata.md` for a finished video — ready to paste into YouTube Studio.

## Title
- 50–60 characters
- Leading keyword first
- Clear value signal (number, outcome, comparison)

**Examples**
- `GTA 6 File Size: How to Free Up 212GB on PS5`
- `GTA 6 Standard vs Ultimate Edition — What You Get`

## Description Structure
```
Line 1: Primary keyword + value (visible before fold)
Line 2: Supporting detail or call to action (visible before fold)
[blank line]
Full explanation...
Timestamps (if applicable)
Sources:
- <official URL>
AI disclosure: Graphics created with AI assistance. All facts sourced from official Rockstar Games documentation.
[Channel links / socials]
```
Max 5000 characters.

## Tags
Mix three tiers:
- **Exact-match**: `gta 6`, `gta vi`, `grand theft auto 6`
- **Broad**: `gta 6 tips`, `gaming 2026`, `ps5 games`
- **Long-tail**: `how much storage does gta 6 need`, `gta vi preload ps5`

## Thumbnail Concept
- Bold, readable text at thumbnail size (1280×720)
- High-contrast — dark bg + bright accent
- No misleading imagery or manufactured shock
- Suggest 2–3 layout variants

## Chapters (videos > 2 min)
```
0:00 Intro
0:12 The numbers
0:45 How to free up space
1:30 Which edition to buy
```

## Never
- Promise views, rankings, or monetization timelines
- Use clickbait titles that contradict the video content

## Output
Save to `exports/<topic>/metadata.md`
