# Clip rights checker

Inventory every third-party asset with source URL, creator, exact file or excerpt, intended edit, and intended platform. Find the license or explicit permission from the rights holder; record attribution, commercial-use, modification, platform, geographic and expiry terms. Check game publisher content policies when gameplay is involved. Treat missing or unclear terms as uncleared, and suggest owned footage or an original replacement.

Produce a clearance table with evidence links and a status of cleared, conditional, or uncleared. State the exact conditions and attribution wording. Do not treat “found online,” credit alone, or a fair-use guess as permission. Escalate genuinely ambiguous legal questions for human review; do not upload an uncleared asset.
