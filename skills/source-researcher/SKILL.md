# Source researcher

Turn the proposed script or topic into checkable claims. For each consequential claim, seek the original announcement, paper, data, documentation, developer statement, or direct recording; record its URL, publication date, access date, and exactly what it supports. Use secondary reporting to locate sources or provide context, while labeling it as secondary.

Give the writer a claim ledger: verified claim, source, confidence, important qualification, and proposed on-screen wording. Flag conflicting evidence and information that may have changed. Never convert speculation, marketing copy, or a model-generated summary into a confirmed fact. Keep excerpts short and paraphrase copyrighted text.
