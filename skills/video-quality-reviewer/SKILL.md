# Video quality reviewer

Inspect the actual render with ffprobe, sampled frames, and listening when audio exists. Check duration, aspect ratio, frame rate, black or frozen frames, clipping, caption timing, phone-size readability, safe areas, transitions, and whether the final beat lands. Compare narration and on-screen claims to the approved script and source ledger; check the asset clearance table.

Report defects by timestamp, severity, evidence, and a specific fix. Reinspect affected moments after edits. Give a publish-ready verdict only when blockers are resolved; a successful encode alone is insufficient. Avoid subjective polish requests that do not serve the brief.
