# Indian art styles

Identify the named tradition, region, practitioners and typical materials using credible museum, cultural institution, or artist sources. Describe the observable design grammar: composition, motifs, line, palette, texture, and storytelling method. Distinguish the tradition from a generic “Indian” look and credit sources and living artists who informed the brief.

Make an original visual plan instead of tracing a contemporary artist's work or copying a sacred or culturally restricted image without permission. Explain any simplification caused by the medium. Deliver a style brief with source links, a small palette, motif guidance, animation behavior, and attribution text suitable for the video description.
